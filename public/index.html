Core Capabilities

Engineered for Zero-Dispensing-Error Compliance

MediGuard combines proactive algorithmic date checks with rigorous administrator controls to ensure patients never receive degraded or expired medications.

Automated Expiry Surveillance

Calculates exact days remaining dynamically against the current date, categorizing stocks into Safe, Expiring Soon, and Expired without manual spreadsheet checks.

Instant Quarantine Alerts

Critical red warning banners and flashing status badges alert dispensing staff immediately when an item crosses its shelf-life boundary.

Batch & Shelf Coordinates

Pinpoints every medicine by batch number, storage location (e.g. Shelf A-02, Cold Storage B), supplier provenance, and manufacturing date.

Audit-Ready CSV & Print

Export complete inventory inspection sheets as structured CSV logs or generate printable pharmacy audit sheets formatted for healthcare inspectors.

Operational Workflow

How MediGuard Safeguards Your Pharmacy

1
Ingest & Register Batch

Log new medicine consignments with batch code, category, storage coordinates, manufacturing date, and expiry date via the Admin portal.

2
Autonomous 24/7 Surveillance

Backend algorithms compute countdowns each day, sorting stock into 30-day early warnings and instant expired quarantine buckets.

3
Action, Quarantine & Audit

Staff isolate flagged drugs, initiate vendor returns or authorized hazardous medical waste disposal, and export signed audit trails.

Defense In Depth

Enterprise Grade Security Controls

Designed to maintain data integrity and regulatory compliance under hospital security standards.

  • Session-Backed Administrator Authentication
    Only authenticated pharmacists can register, modify, or delete inventory records.
  • Guest Read-Only Protection
    Public visitors can inspect system readiness, but destructive mutations are strictly gated.
  • Strict MongoDB Schema Validation
    Ensures mandatory batch uniqueness, non-negative quantities, and chronological date integrity.
